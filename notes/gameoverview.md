For the 2026-2027 the game we are playing is Override. Override is played on a 12’ x 12’ with 2 alliances, red and blue. The goal  of the game is to outscore the opposing team in a 2 minute match in which there is a 15 second autonomous period and a 1:45 driver control period. 

The main way to score in this game is to stack cups and pins on goals, setting toggles to your alliances color and ending the match with your bot in the midfield.

Unlike last years game, Pushback, this yeas game is not a cycling game. It is a game of strategy and stacking. To win you must be good at not only stacking pins but being able to keep track of time and be able to shut down opponents.

12 points awarded to the team who wins in the autonomous period. If the autonomous period ends in a tie, 6 points are awared to both teams. There are multiple types of pins with color combinations being yellow-yellow, yellow-blue, yellow-red and red-blue with there being 19, 20, 20 and 4 of each colored pins respectively. Red and blue pins earn 5 points for their alliance respectively no matter where on the field they are. Yellow pins can earch up to 10 points depending on the color of the toggle in the quadrant where the yellow pin is placed. When the toggle color showing is yellow, the pins in that quadrant count for 0 points earned for both alliances. Each cup has a transparent and solid side.
