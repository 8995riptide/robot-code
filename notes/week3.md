## Overview of the week

### Summary  
Almost everything is getting finished for the competition on Oct 23\. One of the drivetrains motors broke so we had to fix that. The lift was close to not being done due to us debating whether or not to build a lift with steel but we decided to build it anyway because we would much rather have a shabby lift than no lift at all. Also, instead of building a DR4B, we are build a 4 bar. The claw is doing amazing but the “easy” part, attaching the toggles turned out to be a bit more of a challenge than we predicted. The code is finalized but due to the fact that our bot wasn’t complete, we werent able to test it.

### Drivetrain  
One of the motors on the drive train fell apart and stopped working. We never did figure out why it did that. Due to that we had to reattach the motor. Last week we were having issues where one side of the drivetrain was faster than the other but we didn’t mention it. Turns out that one of the motors were red instead of matching the others so we had to fix that. That clarified most of the issues. Now our bot can go faster. Also, the spacing of the gears got messed up because when fixing the motor someone dropped all of the spacers on the ground instead of keeping it on the axle meaning we had to remember and remake all of the spacing. It wasn’t too hard but it meant that we had to basically remake half of our drivetrain.

### Claw \+ Toggles  
The claw was tested and modified multiple times until it was perfect. Surprisingly, the supposedly easy part, attaching the toggles, wasn’t as simple as we thought it would be. Firstly, when testing, the toggles were too low meaning the toggles would hit the wall below the toggles. Then, once we fixed the height of the bars to around 13.75’, the toggles kept on sliding off of the toggle instead of flipping it. It was scraping the toggle but not flipping it. We left around 20 scratches on the toggle bar trying to test. The issue was that, due to the fact that the screww which was sticking off at the end of the stand off screw combo, it wasn’t wide enough to flip the toggle. Instead of using a stand-off, we put a bunch of spacers on the screw and made it like a fat stand off. After figuring out all of the height reuirments, we attached it to the 4 bar so that raising the 4 bar would cause it to flip toggles.
